# Finance Phase 2 — payment control, month-end close, bank and payment packs

**Status (31 Oct 2026):** PROPOSED in a draft PR on branch `claude/project-thread-gmzh82`. Not merged, not deployed, no migration run, `FINANCE_ROLLOUT` not set anywhere. Mock data only.

Fauzan confirmed on 31 Oct 2026 that "Phase 2" is the next department rollout after the IT pilot: Finance. This is the first slice of it. It builds on the Finance monitor that already exists (27 Oct 2026, rule FIN-WATCH: the per-company board of SQL Account freshness, bank reconciliation, close, integrity tests, posting gaps, and the auditor and tax agent papers).

## Ground rules kept
- **SQL Account stays the system of financial record** (V6). Prima never posts to it and never writes its tables.
- **The bank portal stays where money moves.** Prima approves and records the bank's reference; it never pays anything and holds no bank passwords, TACs or full account numbers (the server refuses text that looks like them).
- **Every approval has at least a comment** (Fauzan, 31 Oct 2026). Returns, cancellations and reopenings need a reason of at least 10 characters.
- **The CEO has full authority alongside the Finance Director** (Fauzan, relayed 31 Oct 2026), but is bound by the same separation of duties as everyone else.
- **Power BI is left out**; it comes in the last phase.

## What it adds

### 1. Payment control (the Phase 2 gate: one user may not authorise and pay the same transaction)
| Step | Who | Must not be |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare (payee, purpose, amount, due date, company bank account, SQL Account document number, at least one supporting document) | Anyone in the company's Finance team, the Finance Director, the CEO | — |
| Review (comment needed) | Team deputy or team leader, the Finance Director, the CEO | the preparer |
| Approve (comment needed) | **By subject** (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026: only the FD, the CEO and the COO approve anything). **Finance** payments: the Finance Director or the CEO, at any amount. Payments for the departments the **COO** monitors (Operations, IT, Foreign Worker Management, Procurement & Inventory, Transport, Goodwill Empire): the COO or the CEO, never the FD; the COO sees only those, in his own *Payments to approve* screen, and can only approve or return them. Team leaders and deputies review, never approve. The CEO is also reminded of payments of RM500,000 or more | the preparer or the reviewer |
| Record release (bank reference needed) | Anyone in the team, the Finance Director, the CEO | the approver |
| Return (reason) | Whoever may review or approve at that step | — |
| Cancel (reason) | The preparer, the team leader, the Finance Director, the CEO | — |

A payment returned to the preparer is resubmitted by them. A second payment with the same SQL Account document, or the same payee and amount, is refused as a likely duplicate until the first is cancelled.

### 2. Month-end close with the Finance Director's approval ("Approved financials")
For last month, per company. The close cannot be sent for review while any of these hold:
- no figures from SQL Account in the last 48 hours (Unknown, never green);
- the month is not closed in SQL Account;
- any bank account is not reconciled to the month end in SQL Account, or its SQL Account reconciliation report is not attached;
- FS-01, FS-02 or FS-06 is not run or not exactly RM0.00 (V6).

Every other exception (posting gaps; FS-07/FS-08 differences) must be explained before the close is sent for review. The deputy or leader reviews (not the preparer); the Finance Director or the CEO approves (neither the preparer nor the reviewer), and only once the month's bank pack is reviewed. Reopening needs a reason.

### 3. Bank pack and payment pack
Two new monthly packs beside the auditor and tax agent papers, generated per company:
- **Bank pack** (due the 10th): the statement and the SQL Account reconciliation for every bank account, the unpresented cheques and uncredited deposits list, and the signed reconciliation summary.
- **Payment pack** (due the 15th): the SQL Account payment voucher listing, invoices and Prima approvals for every payment, bank release confirmations, and the EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB and HRD Corp receipts.

For all four packs: data entry prepares, the deputy or leader reviews, the leader or Finance Director (or the CEO) sends. **New:** nobody reviews or sends a paper they prepared, and reviewing and sending need a comment.

### 4. Who can sign in
The IT pilot gate (rule PILOT-IT) is unchanged. A new switch `FINANCE_ROLLOUT=true` on the API also admits the Finance Director and every Finance team member. It is **off by default**; only Arisha main sets it, and only after the CEO approves the Finance go-live. `LEADERSHIP_ONBOARDING_ONLY` still applies on top of it.

### 5. Link to the SharePoint request and the Teams approval
Staff keep requesting in SharePoint and approving in Teams. On any open payment, a Finance user links the request (`https` link on `sharepoint.com`, `teams.microsoft.com`, `teams.cloud.microsoft` only) and copies each approver's decision **with their comment**. A decision with no comment is refused ("ask them to add one in Teams"), which enforces the rule that approvers always comment. A rejection in Teams stops the approval in Prima. This is a link and a copy, not an integration: Prima does not read Teams or SharePoint yet (that needs Microsoft Graph permissions and the CEO's consent, a later PR).

### 6. Paid to the right person
- **Payee master per company.** Name, type, registration number, bank, **last 4 digits only**, the account name the bank shows. New payments must pick a payee from it; blocked payees cannot be used.
- **Verification by call-back.** Someone other than the person who entered the details verifies them: call-back on a number from the company's own records (never the number on the request), a bank letter, or the bank portal/DuitNow name check. The number's source is recorded.
- **Change of bank account** sends the payee back to "To verify" and holds its open payments. Approval and release are refused until it is verified again; a payment approved before the change cannot be released ("Return it for a fresh approval").
- **At release** the person recording it types the last 4 digits and the account name the bank portal shows. Prima stops the release if either differs from the verified payee.
- **After payment** the bank statement line is matched to the payment, and Prima flags a line whose description does not mention the payee.

### 7. Bank statements
Each month, for each bank account, the statement is loaded as CSV from the bank portal (date, description, reference, amount; money out negative), with opening and closing balances; Prima refuses a file that does not add up. Money out is matched automatically to released payments by amount and bank reference (or amount and date within 3 days), or by hand with a comment. Money out with no approved Prima payment, and payments released 5+ days ago that are not on a statement, become close exceptions that must be explained. Only senior staff replace a loaded statement.

### 8. Monthly ageing
By the 10th, the team records the Customer Aging and Supplier Aging totals from SQL Account (not due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, over 90) with debtors and creditors from the ledger and the report file. A deputy or leader reviews it (not the preparer). No ageing, or an unreviewed one, blocks the close; a difference from the ledger is a close exception.

### 9. SST to Customs
Security services are taxable service (First Schedule, Group G), 8% since 1 March 2024. Per company: registered or not, SST number, the month the two-month taxable periods start, taxable services over the last 12 months (a warning if over the RM500,000 threshold and not registered). For the last three ended periods: prepare (value of taxable services and the tax declared, from the SQL Account SST listing/SST-02 draft; a difference from value × 8% over RM1 needs a reason), review (not the preparer), record the MySST filing (acknowledgement number and date), and link the payment to Customs, which must be a normal Prima payment to a tax payee for **exactly** the declared tax. Late returns and the late payment penalty (10% for the first 30 days, then +15%, +15%, up to 40%) are shown.

### 10. Monthly audit file
A third monthly pack (due the 20th) with what the external auditor asks for at year end, kept up every month: trial balance and GL, ageing with subsequent receipts, fixed asset register and additions, payroll reconciliation to EPF/SOCSO/EIS/PCB, SST returns and payments, accruals and prepayments, new contracts and leases, board minutes and resolutions, intercompany balances agreed, and customer balance confirmations for big debtors. The Checks tab also shows the year-end deadlines: audited accounts circulated within 6 months of year end (Companies Act 2016 s.258), lodged with SSM within 30 days after (s.259), Form C within 7 months.

### 11. Budget and forecast
One budget per company per year by line (revenue, guard pay, staff pay, statutory, premises, vehicles, equipment, insurance, other expenses, finance cost, tax, capital spending), with written assumptions. A deputy, leader, the FD or the CEO drafts; the FD or the CEO approves (not the preparer) with a comment. **The approved budget is not changed;** after each close the team records the month's actuals from the SQL Account P&L and re-forecasts the year (by the 20th) with a note on what changed. Differences of RM5,000 and 10% or more against budget must be explained.

### 12. e-Invoice (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026: "Malaysia now needs e-invoicing; it is connected with SQL Account")
SQL Account's MyInvois module sends sales invoices, cash sales, credit and debit notes and self-billed purchase documents to LHDN, shows Valid, Invalid, Submitting (stuck) and Cancelled, builds the monthly consolidated e-Invoice and lists everything in MyInvois Transaction. Prima does not send e-Invoices; it checks every month that SQL Account did (rule `FIN-EINV`, data in `fin_status.einvoice`):
- **Set-up per company** (team leader, FD or CEO): the company's own turnover, required or not, basis, start date, sent via SQL Account, the Portal or another system. Since 1 Sep 2026 companies below RM3 million turnover are exempt. **Fauzan decided (1 Nov 2026) that each company is tested on its own turnover, not group sales**; at RM3 million or more Prima refuses "not required".
- **Monthly check by the 10th** (recorded by the team, reviewed by someone else): sales documents issued, valid, invalid, stuck, not submitted (must add up); the consolidated e-Invoice UUID and date (due by the 7th); single sales above RM10,000 that went into the consolidated e-Invoice; self-billed e-Invoices needed and validated (foreign suppliers and agents); supplier invoices posted without a validated e-Invoice; cancellations after 72 hours.
- **The close is blocked** without the check, without its review, while any sales document is not validated, or when the consolidated e-Invoice is missing. A late consolidated e-Invoice, big sales consolidated, missing self-billed e-Invoices, supplier invoices without e-Invoices and late cancellations must be explained.
- **Payments:** a supplier payment can carry the supplier's e-Invoice UUID; supplier payments without one are listed on the Checks tab ("ask the supplier for its validated e-Invoice before paying").
- Penalty after the relaxation period: ITA s.120(1)(d), RM200 to RM20,000 per instance, up to 6 months' jail, or both.

### 13. Invoice problems and bad debt (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026)
Problem: an invoice goes to a payer or site that has changed, nobody pays it, and the ageing shows it only after 3 months. Rule `FIN-AR`, data in `fin_status.arCases` and `fin_status.auditedTo`:
- **A case per stuck invoice** (invoice number, date, customer, amount, unpaid, e-Invoice UUID; wrong payer, site changed, dispute, refused, insolvent, other). Every invoice unpaid 90 days or more needs one: the close shows the ageing's over-90 amount not covered by cases as an exception. Each case is followed up (reminder, call, letter of demand, lawyer) at least every 30 days or by the date set; overdue follow-ups are close exceptions.
- **Never delete an invoice.** Wrong payer or site: a credit note in SQL Account (and its e-Invoice) plus a new invoice to the right payer, approved by someone other than the requester. Cancelling in MyInvois is allowed only for a validated e-Invoice within 72 hours.
- **Customer will not pay:** doubtful debt provision (approved by deputy, leader, FD or CEO, not the requester), then **bad debt write-off** after 180 days of recovery steps (or earlier if insolvent), with at least 2 pieces of evidence, the SQL Account credit note or journal, and approval by the **Finance Director or the CEO only**, never the requester. Partial payments reduce the unpaid amount.
- **Government and other slow payers** (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026: some government sites pay after 9 months): reason "Government, pays late". After 90 days every overdue invoice is followed up at least every month until paid. The **Finance Director** is told on their home screen (invoices over 90 days with no follow-up this month, and companies whose overdue invoices they have not yet reviewed this month) and signs off each company's overdue list every month with a comment; with any invoice lacking a follow-up, the comment must say what the team was told.
- **SST (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026):** the group pays service tax when the customer's payment is received, not on invoice, so bad debt relief should rarely be needed. When bad debt relief or a refund of a wrong payment is needed, Prima keeps a **Customs claim**: the full document pack (at least 3 documents), prepared by one person, submitted to Customs by a team leader, the FD or the CEO who did not prepare it (with the Customs reference), and Customs' answer (approved amount or rejected). Recovery is usually very hard.
- **Audited years:** the latest signed audit is recorded (leader, FD or CEO; cannot go backwards). A credit note for an invoice in an audited year must be dated in the current open month; the screen tells the team to inform the auditor, and the FD decides with the auditor whether a prior-year restatement is needed (MFRS 108 / MPERS section 10).

### Automatic bank reconciliation with SQL Account (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026)
"Staff upload the bank statement and the system checks." Under Finance > Checks:
1. Staff load the month's bank statement (CSV from the bank portal) as before.
2. Staff paste SQL Account's ledger for the same bank account (GL Ledger or Cash Book export: date, document no, description, debit, credit) with its opening and closing balance; Prima refuses an export that does not add up.
3. Prima pairs every line: by reference (the SQL document or cheque number on the bank line, or the bank reference in the SQL entry) with the same amount; then by the same amount within the match window when there is one candidate each way; then a single bank line against several SQL entries of one day that add up to it (payroll or supplier batches, receipts banked together); and last month's bank items posted late in SQL Account.
4. What is left is listed: payments in SQL Account not yet at the bank, deposits not yet credited, and bank items not in SQL Account (charges, interest, standing orders). Items outstanding more than 60 days are flagged. Last month's outstanding items carry forward automatically.
5. The balance is proved: bank closing + deposits not yet credited − payments not yet presented = SQL closing + bank items not yet in SQL. It must be RM0.00.
6. Bank-only items are explained with their SQL posting reference, or matched by hand (amounts must agree, comment needed). Someone other than whoever ran it reviews it. Whoever released payments from that account that month cannot run it.
7. At the close, a reviewed Prima reconciliation that balances replaces the separate SQL Account reconciliation report for that account; a reconciliation that is off, unexplained or unreviewed blocks the close. A reviewed one is redone only by the FD or the CEO.
SQL Account is not connected: the ledger is an export staff paste in. A read-only connector (E62) would fetch it directly later.

### Automation by system rules (Fauzan, 1 Nov 2026: "1. Ok 2. Ok 3. Ok 4. ... 5. Yes")
Prima prepares, matches, checks and reminds; it never approves. List and later candidates: `/mnt/project-files/plans/finance-automation-by-rules.md` (project files). Built:
1. **Packs filled by Prima.** A bank, payment or monthly audit-file item is shown as prepared by "Prima (automatic)", with what it contains, when Prima holds everything it needs: the loaded statement; a reviewed Prima reconciliation balanced to RM0.00 (and, with all of them, the outstanding items list); every September release with its approval comment, documents and bank reference; the reviewed ageing; the reviewed and filed SST-02 ending that month. It is saved only when someone acts on it, so an untouched one follows the data. A person still reviews (with a comment) and sends it. The SQL Account voucher listing, statutory receipts and signed summary stay with staff.
2. **Lapsed approvals return themselves.** On the lapse day the payment is returned to the preparer with a system step (`prima:auto`) saying why; saved and logged (`finance.payment.lapsed`) the first time anyone acts on the books. Release and a second return are refused with the reason; the preparer is told on home and resubmits for a fresh review and approval.
3. **Slow steps escalate.** The FD's and the CEO's home lists payments waiting more than `escalateAfterDays` (default 3) for review or for approval, and closes prepared or reviewed but not approved by `closeApproveByDay` (default 15). The FD sets both in Settings.
4. **SST on money received** (Fauzan: "the sst amount is 8% from billing amount without sst ... 8/108 x payment receive"). The return is prepared from the money received for taxable services, SST included: tax = 8/108 of it, taxable value = the rest. It must agree with the SST on the invoices those receipts paid (8% of the bill) and the declared tax must equal it, each within RM1 or explained (part or short payments, wrong SST on an invoice). Older returns prepared on value x rate still read.
5. **Variance owner and due day.** Every variance over the threshold has an owner (the company's team leader) and a due day (`varianceByDay` of the month after, default 15); the leader is told on home, late ones go to the FD and the CEO. The FD or the CEO accepts each explanation with a comment (`acceptVariance`), never their own.
Tests: `packages/core/test/finance-automation.test.ts` (5). Defaults 3 / 15 / 15 days are proposals for the FD to set.

### Regular payments, guarantees and hostel bills (Fauzan, 2 Nov 2026)

Fauzan asked what Finance still missed: hostel payment, hire purchase, bills, building loan, PCB, zakat, then insurance, IG and BG ("IG can claim back the cash collateral when the IG is expired or released from client").
