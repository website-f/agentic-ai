## ACT (`act`)

**Status:** Main, extended heavily (#55).

**Purpose.** ACT (Audit, Compliance and Transformation): 1 Director and 2 teams, each with 1 Auditor and 1 Enforcement
officer. ACT monitors all compliance, audits every workflow, checks discrepancies, finds the solution and leads the
improvement. The owner fixes, the Auditor verifies, Enforcement follows up. Overdue findings are shown to the next level,
never punished.

**Who sees it.** ACT staff and group leaders; owners (any PWVSB staff or company manager) see the findings they must fix.
The ACT head sees everything, to audit it.

**Sections.**

| Section | What it does | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| **Compliance monitor** | Every workstream's compliance state; **Raise a finding** | ACT |
| **Findings** (and **My findings** for owners) | Owner: **Save the plan** (cause, fix, done by), **Mark fixed** with proof. ACT: **Record follow-up**, **Fix works, close**, **Not fixed**. Fix within 14/30/60 days by severity; overdue goes to the Chief of Staff, COO (14 days), CEO (30 days). Auditors and Enforcement act only on their own team's findings | Owner, Auditor, Enforcement, Director |
| **Workflow audit plan** | High-risk workflows every 6 months, others yearly; **Save audit** | ACT |
| **Improvements** | **Propose an improvement**; COO or CEO **Approve**; **Record result**; **Done** | ACT, COO, CEO |
| **Work given to the teams** | The Director distributes audits and report reviews; the team's Auditor **Record done** | Director, Auditors |
| **Monthly reports** | Every department's and company's monthly report from the Director Office list; ACT checks each; the Director sets each department's report form (**Save form**) | ACT |
| **Workflow timing** | After reviewing a workflow, the Director sets how long each step may take, with a walk-through; a field check every 6 months of what really happens (**Add a step**, **Save timing**, **Record field check**) | Director |
| **Record patterns to check** *(candidate)* | Fraud-risk patterns over the last 90 days (for example clock-ins by different people from the same place within minutes); **Open investigation** | ACT |
| **Investigations** *(candidate)* | **Open file**, **Add evidence**, **Add note**, **Close investigation**. Seen only by ACT; evidence kept with its SHA-256 and every open, add, view and close logged. The patterns are fixed checks on records already in Prima, never AI and never an accusation; nobody is disciplined automatically | ACT |
| Guide cards | Read-only help for each ACT task | |

## Speak up and assurance (`grc`)

**Status:** Main.

**Purpose.** Everyone can report wrongdoing (anonymously if they wish) and declare gifts. Each company proves its
obligations and internal audit checks the proof; no proof is Unknown, never green. A report never reaches anyone it
names.

**Who sees it.** Everyone (guards and supervisors included) for reporting and gifts; handlers, ACT and leaders for the rest.

**Sections.**
- **Report wrongdoing**: **Send report**, **Make another report**; **Check a report** with the reference.
- **Gifts and hospitality**: **Declare a gift**; above the limit a decision is needed.
- **What each company must prove**: **Record proof**; ACT **Accept proof** or **Reject**.
- **Site risk assessments (HIRARC)**: **Record**.
- **Reports you handle**: **Acknowledge** (within 7 days, *proposed*), **Investigate**, **Add note**, **Propose closing**.
- **Closures for a second handler to agree**: **Agree and close** or **Send back**.

## Audit trail (`audit`)

**Status:** Main.

**Purpose.** The hash-chained log of every action: who, when, which company, what, and a summary (old and new values for
record edits). Any change to history breaks the chain and can be detected.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, ACT, Arms Control and company managers (their own company).
