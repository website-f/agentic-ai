# Screens: central admin, governance, insurance, memos and PDPA

Status key: **Main**, **Candidate (#nn)**, **Main, extended (#nn)**. See [screens index](README.md).

---

## Licences (`compliance`)

**Status:** Main.

**Purpose.** Each company's licences and registrations (KDN, MOF, SSM, insurance and others) with expiry state.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, PWVSB staff with a grant, company managers and admin executives.

**What is on it.** Company licences, filtered to **Needs action** or **All**. Add or edit a licence through the record
form (the company keeps it). An expired security licence blocks new contracts and bids.

---

## Renewals (`renewals`)

**Status:** Candidate (#52, Central Admin 5C).

**Purpose.** Each company renews its own licences, registrations, majlis licences, vendor registrations and ISO
certificates; Central Admin (the Deputy Chief of Staff) monitors them all and confirms each new expiry. Prima lodges and
pays nothing.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, ACT, company managers and admin executives.

**Steps.** **Open renewal** (opened ahead: KDN 120 days, MOF 90, insurance 90, SSM 60); **Add paper** from the starting
checklist; **Sign the application** (company manager, never the preparer); **Record fee** (paid through Finance);
**Record submission**; **Agency note** (follow-up every 7 days); **Record new certificate**; Central Admin **Confirm new
expiry** or **Query it** / **Send back**. Late renewals step up at 0, 14 and 30 days.

## Company secretary (`corporate`)

**Status:** Main, extended (#52 company files, panel firms, delegations, legal holds).

**Purpose.** Each company keeps its own licensed secretary, filings and board. Head office Corporate Admin (the Deputy
Chief of Staff) records each filing; the company and its secretary view them. Any change to a company's corporate
structure needs PWVSB approval.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, legal (audit and legal tabs only), the company secretary (view only), company managers and
admin executives.

**Sections.**

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **SSM filings** | Each filing with **Papers ready for the secretary**, **Secretary lodged it**, **Record SSM receipt**; annual return within 30 days; financial statements circulated within 6 months of year end |
| **Corporate changes** | The company **Ask PWVSB to approve a change** (citing the signed clause); the Deputy Chief of Staff **Approve**s or **Reject**s with a reason; **Record board resolution**; **Send to PWVSB** |
| **Companies** | Each company's secretary, board and filing status |
| **Company files** *(candidate, 5B)* | One corporate record per company: directors, shareholders, beneficial owners, constitution, key papers. The Head of Company or Central Admin drafts; the Deputy Chief of Staff checks it against the SSM extract (**Matches SSM** or **Send back with differences**) and never checks their own draft |
| **Audit and legal: Audit** | Statutory audit with a panel firm: **Planning meeting held**, **Handed over**, adjustments (**Add adjustment**, **Agree** by the manager), **Report signed**. Prima never signs or files |
| **Audit and legal: Legal matters** | PWVSB legal **Open a matter** with the company and follows it: **Save update**, **Save fee**, **Close matter** |
| **Panel firms** *(candidate, 5D)* | Central Admin keeps audit and secretary firms; legal keeps law firms. **Add firm**, **Record review**, **Take off the panel**; yearly budget (**Propose budget**, FD **Agree budget**); invoices keyed once and checked by someone else (**Record invoice**, **Confirm service**, **Link SQL Account entry**, **Pass to Finance** or **Query**). Law fees come from the legal matters. Prima never pays |
| **Delegation and succession** *(candidate)* | Critical posts and who could step in (**Reviewed**, **Add critical post**); delegation lines: who acts for whom, over what, from when to when and why. **Record evidence** (competency; board resolution for succession), then one leader (CEO, COO or Chief of Staff) **Approve**s, never the recorder or the delegate. **Ask IT for access** / **Ask IT to remove access**, **Hand over work** / **Hand work back**, **End**, **Revoke** |
| **Legal holds** *(candidate)* | Legal or the Deputy Chief of Staff **Place hold** on a record for an open legal matter: while it stands, the database keeps every earlier version and refuses to delete it. Staff work is not stopped and staff are not told. **Release hold**. Seen only by legal and group leaders |

## Mandates (`governance`)

**Status:** Main.

**Purpose.** The authority under which PWVSB acts in each company. PWVSB acts only under a signed management agreement or
board mandate that the company's board confirmed and PWVSB legal verified.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, legal, the Chief of Staff's office and company managers.

**What is on it.** **Management agreements and board mandates** (**Register a mandate**, **Board confirmed it**, **Signed
copy verified**, **Mark ended**; warned 90 days before ending); **What PWVSB may do, by company** (areas with no live
mandate show Unknown, never covered); **Controlled by the company, monitored by PWVSB**; **Who approves what**.

## Insurance (`insurance`)

**Status:** Main, extended (#52 claim steps).

**Purpose.** The Chief of Staff's insurance section: premiums, every insurance matter and guarantees for every company.
The section chases; each company's board and Finance pay, sign and settle.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, the Chief of Staff's office, company managers and admin executives.

**Tabs.** **To chase**; **Policies** (renewal quotes from 60 days, *proposed*); **Premiums** (unpaid overdue, due soon,
paid); **Claims** with steps each recorded by a person with its reference (**Record insurer told**, **Add document**,
**Sent for assessment**, **Record offer**, **Accept offer for the company**, **Record money received**, **Record
rejection**, **Appeal (once)**); **Guarantees and bonds** (valid to contract end, extend before contract ends, ask for
release, released); **By company**. Prima never contacts the insurer.

## Memos (`memos`)

**Status:** Main.

**Purpose.** Official memos.

**Who sees it.** Everyone (guards and officers see memos addressed to them).

**Rules and actions.** Only the **Chief of Staff** publishes memos to all staff; only the **COO** publishes memos to all
guards and officers (**Publish**). Readers press **I have read this**; the publisher sees who has read it. Unread memos
appear on the reader's home.

## PDPA (`pdpa`)

**Status:** Main.

**Purpose.** The PDPA officer manages every personal data matter, monitored by the Operations Director.

**Who sees it.** Group leaders, the PDPA officer, and the Operations Director.

**Tabs.**
- **Cases**: breaches and other matters (**Open a case**). Breaches go to the PDP Commissioner within 72 hours, and to
  affected people within 7 days of that when harm is significant. A late notice shows on the CEO's home.
- **Products**: products sold or rented to clients, their personal-data details and certificates (MySTI, SIRIM, MCMC or
  others) with how to renew (**Add product**, **Add certificate**; reminders 90 and 30 days).
- **Proposal check**: the Operations Director ticks the products in a proposal and sees whether it can go to the client.
- **Data requests**: people's requests about their own data; **Identity checked** first; answered within 21 days;
  **Refuse on this ground** only on the Act's grounds.
- **DPO register**: each company's data protection officer, notified to the Commissioner within 21 days.
