## Controlled boundary
OM requests support manually outside Prima. CEO approves named guards, exact amounts and payment occasions manually and sends the approved list to DOA. No guard, OM or staff request portal; no automatic new-guard eligibility or policy cap. DOA maintains a separate controlled Excel workbook. Prima supplies a scoped, minimal, read-only guard master and receives a versioned approved-list handover. Imported names/company/site come from current authoritative records, never free text; guard must be active with that site in the latest published roster before the occasion or the next published roster afterwards. A day-off payment does not require an invented worked shift. Missing/stale roster requires authorized source correction, not OM re-keying guard identity.

DOA submits evidence files for each manual OM request and CEO approval, their identities and timestamps, amount, occasion and workbook reference. Intake validates current requester site/company scope and approver company scope; DOA attests that the evidence actually covers that named guard, amount and occasion. The system records evidence, not an electronic approval or proof of payout. Evidence authenticity and historical role verification remain a human control.

Rows are immutable. Replacement before cash preparation requires an explicit superseded row and different approval evidence; afterwards correction is a Finance exception requiring further design. Duplicate batch, guard/occasion and reused approval identities fail atomically. Finance records preparation and custody, then a single actual payment no greater than the approved amount. DOA uploads a transaction-specific signed receipt after payout and attests signer, payment reference and amount; stored signatures cannot be selected. Finance records return and reconciliation with evidence. Completion is blocked until required payment evidence, reconciliation and supplemental payroll/statutory accounting are recorded.
