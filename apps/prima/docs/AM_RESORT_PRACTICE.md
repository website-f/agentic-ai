# How hotels and resorts are usually run, and what Prima does

Research note for Fauzan, 5 Nov 2026. It covers what a hotel's property management system (PMS) normally does, Malaysian rules that affect a resort, what Prima now does, and what is still left out. The sources are secondary, such as vendor guides and news reports. Finance and the Asset Director must confirm the legal and tax points with Customs, the state and MOTAC before real use.

## What hotels normally do

| Area | Usual practice | Prima now |
|---|---|---|
| Reservations | Room inventory by type and night, never overbooked unless the policy allows it; holds and deposits; group blocks | Yes: 14-night grid, resort lock against double selling, hold of 3 days, 30% deposit, group bookings billed to a company |
| Rates | Approved rate list; discounts need a reason | Yes: the Asset Director sets the list; a lower price needs a reason |
| Front desk | Check-in takes the guest's ID and assigns room numbers; check-out settles the bill | Yes: ID type, number and nationality; room numbers; full payment before check-out |
| Guest register | Required by law for an inn | Yes: kept on the booking, with the ID visible to the team only |
| Housekeeping | Room status Dirty, Clean, Inspected; Out of Order rooms are removed from what can be sold | Yes: dirty, cleaned, inspected; out of order by dates with a reason; no guest in a dirty room; cleaner cannot inspect own room |
| Folio (guest bill) | Room nights plus extras (food, extra bed, activities), taxes and fees; corrections as minus lines with a reason | Yes |
| Night audit / daily report | Check arrivals, departures and no-shows; balance the day's payments; occupancy, ADR (average daily rate), RevPAR (revenue per available room) | Yes, as a daily report on screen |
| Payments to bank | Cash and card takings reconciled with the bank | Yes: Finance confirms each payment in the bank |
| Channel manager / online travel agents (Booking.com, Agoda) | Syncs rooms and prices with online sites | Not built; needs a provider choice and contract |
| Restaurant / POS, key cards, guest profiles and loyalty | Common in larger hotels | Not built; extras are entered by hand |

## Malaysian rules that touch a resort

- **Service tax.** 8% on accommodation once a business's annual taxable turnover is over RM500,000, with a return every two months. Whether each resort company is registered needs Finance to confirm.
- **Tourism tax.** RM10 per room per night for foreign guests only (Tourism Tax Act 2017), with a monthly return to Customs. Malaysians and permanent residents do not pay it.
- **State fee.** Pahang charges a Lestari fee of RM3 per room per night. The hotel collects it and pays it to the state. Both sample resorts are in Pahang.
- **MOTAC registration.** Tourist accommodation premises must be registered with MOTAC under the Tourism Industry Act 1992.
- **Guest register.** The Innkeepers Act 1952 requires an inn to keep a register of guests.
