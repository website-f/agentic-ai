# Sick / hospitalisation leave legal verification

Checked 7 November 2026, read-only official-source research. This is the narrow rule verification for the integration defect, not a general legal certification of payroll.

## Confirmed current rule

For covered ordinary private-sector employees, ordinary sick leave and hospitalisation leave are separate annual entitlements. Ordinary sick days must **not** reduce the 60-day hospitalisation balance under the current rules. The service bands are **less than 2 years: 14 days; 2 years or more but less than 5: 18; 5 years or more: 22**, plus **60 hospitalisation days** per calendar year. At exactly five years, use 22, not 18. Some FAQ tables loosely say “more than 5”; the legislative wording resolves that boundary.

## Scope and implementation cautions

- These rules concern employees under contracts of service. Covered company staff and employed guards should not lose sick-leave entitlement merely because salary exceeds RM4,000: the high-salary exclusions concern specified other benefits, not ordinary sick leave. Do not generalise the rule to contractors/gig workers, public-service leave or excluded employee categories. Domestic employees and certain seafarers have schedule exclusions requiring separate treatment. Preserve jurisdiction and worker-category evidence.
- Hospitalisation is a medical classification, not necessarily physical admission. The statutory proviso covers someone certified ill enough to require hospitalisation even if not admitted. Do not require an admission record as the only possible evidence or consume ordinary sick days first.
- Do not count the same leave day twice. Ordinary/dental sick leave certification, notification rules and exclusions for overlapping maternity/disablement benefits remain separate checks. This verification does not approve automatic rejection without reviewing evidence.
- Statutory sick/hospital leave is separate from the user's RM200 **annual** foreign-worker medical-reimbursement policy and mandatory employer medical obligations.

## Historical records and migration

The current separation must not be silently applied to all historical dates. Before the effective dates above, the former statutory shared60 ceiling existed for then-covered employees, subject to any more generous contractual/company benefit and historic coverage. Do not recalculate or overwrite closed decisions/payroll automatically.

For ordinary current-year2026 balances, separate the buckets: hospital remaining depends on hospital usage, not ordinary sick usage. Ordinary remaining depends on ordinary usage. Keep approved/pending/cancelled/returned treatment consistent with the existing workflow and prevent duplicate consumption.

Use date-effective rule versions and preserve the applied version in calculations. A request spanning an effective date, a jurisdiction transfer, or an imported2025 Sabah/Sarawak balance needs a reviewed transition policy. The sources checked do not settle every within-year transition allocation; do not invent proration or reset a fresh60 days on1May. Flag these cases for HR/legal verification rather than auto-paying/reducing historical entitlement.
