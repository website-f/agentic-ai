# IT department gaps (23 Nov 2026)

Fauzan, 23 Nov 2026: "Do the gap", after the study "IT department in Prima: how it works and the gaps"
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Bi58z6bDijEc8uDHQ3NVn1), all six as recommended. Synthetic data only. Not deployed.

## What changed

| Item | Where | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| B1 a leaver is handed to IT | IT > Joining and leaving > "From HR"; Do today for IT | When HR closes a leaver's Prima access, IT sees the person, every device still assigned to them (by name, in use or in repair), whether the leaver checklist is started and how far, and any Microsoft 365 account still active. IT starts the checklist from the row; it can link the leaver even though their access is closed. Do today: "leaver(s) still hold IT devices" (red) and "joiner(s) or leaver(s) from HR with no IT checklist". The row stays until the checklist is done and no device is with the person. Never automatic: IT records each step with evidence. |
| B2 IT buying stays with IT | `itPurchaseStep`, rule IT-BUY `route: 'it'` | An agreed IT purchase goes to IT quoting (Merdeka IT policy: IT quotes, recommends, receives) whether or not the old pilot switch is on. Before, switching the pilot off sent every IT purchase to the Procurement department. |
| B3 companies see what they rent | IT > My requests > "Our IT devices and bills" (company manager) | Own company only: rented devices (tag, kind, model, status, who has it, where) and the last 24 monthly bills with paid or not. Read only. |
| D1 one door for a new joiner | "From HR" + "Record Microsoft 365 approval"; HR / IT coordination leaves the menu | A new account approved in Accounts and Access (HR Director or CEO) shows for IT, opens the joining checklist from the row and counts as the Microsoft 365 approval: IT records an approved account request that names the Accounts and Access request, with no second line manager or CoS step. Not the preparer, the approver or the person. Once per person. The staged HR-IT intake screen is off the menu; its records stay. |
| D2 IT tenders come from Marketing | IT > Tenders (eP) | Once the CEO or COO starts the Marketing desk, the IT Manager or Lead can no longer add a tender of their own; IT passes a tender it spots to Marketing, and the IT part comes back through "Send the IT part to IT". |
| D3 Finance team for Merdeka | IT screen | A member (leader, deputy, data entry) of the Finance team named for Merdeka's IT purchases (`it_purchase_finance_authorities`) gets the same IT Finance tabs as the FD: Purchases, Device rental, Assets, Polisi IT. The budget check and IT purchase invoice and payment were already theirs. Recording a monthly rental bill's payment still needs the FD: that step is held by two pinned database checks (`it_operations_permission`, `rc_role`) that this change does not alter. |

## Database

New module `db/modules/it-gaps-v1.sql` (not on `db/release-manifest.json`; Fauzan runs it after the merge with
`infra/run-db-migration.sh`). It adds one read-only definer function, `prima.it_company_rentals(company)`, that returns a
few columns of the devices and bills of ONE company, and only to an active company manager holding that company. It adds
no policy to `it_assets` or `it_rental_bills`, so their readiness checks are unchanged. Until it is installed a company
manager sees an empty list, never an error.

D1 and B1 read `account_requests` (module accounts-access-v1) and `it_policy_recs` (module it-policies-v1); before those
are installed the "From HR" list is empty.
